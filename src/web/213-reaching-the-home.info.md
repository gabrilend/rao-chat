# 213-reaching-the-home.js

The page's settings, its WebSocket to the home and its connection test
(issue 403): the lines pairing printed (`189 add <label> --browser`),
read by 190's config rule, with the token from its own password box; one
WebSocket per connection, one 302 frame per binary message, a sealed
ping first; the home-network address with quick-wait, then the public
one with wait (307); the test's words for what a browser can tell apart.
`rememberText` gives the lines without the token.
