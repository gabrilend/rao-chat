# 220-signature.js

A message's signature, read from its text by the owner's one rule (214,
written in 169) in the page: split at the last blank line; one line of at
most 80 characters after it is the signature, otherwise the last word.
Characters are counted from the bytes as 169 counts them.  Graded by
`src/signature/170-signature-examples.txt` (issue 406).
