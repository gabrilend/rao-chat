# 203-rao-chat.html

The web client's page (issue 401): open it from a copy of `src/web/` on
your own computer (planning question 5, 401 decision 1).  Section 9's
frame: the top bar (the home's name, the connection state, the tabs),
the error strip, the room trees on the left, the open room and its
compose line on the right, and the Homes, People and Settings panels over
the room view.  It names every stylesheet and script it uses, all in this
folder, in the order they run; it has no inline script and no colour.
