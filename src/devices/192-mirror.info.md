# 192-mirror.lua

The viewer's folder as a device mirrors it (issue 304), shared by the
home (187) and the device (190): `check_path` (a 32-hex mailbox and room,
301's `state`, `.tree`, `.room` and `<written>-<id>` names, nothing
else), `listing` (one `find` of the layout), `rooms`, `entries`, the
digest of a room up to a position, the `catch-up` positions text, and
`write` (under a temporary name, then renamed).
